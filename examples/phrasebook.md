# Senel phrasebook

Every phrase below is built from ordinary roots — Senel has no set-phrase vocabulary,
so greetings are just short sentences. Check any line with
`python3 senel.py gloss "<phrase>"`.

## Meeting people

| Senel | Literally | English |
|---|---|---|
| `Pe sin fen.` | may you be-joyful | Hello. |
| `Pe yen fin.` | may the day be-good | Good day. |
| `Sin es fin he?` | you are good? | How are you? |
| `Min es fin so.` | I am good [internal] | I'm well. |
| `Sul en sin es um nam he?` | name of you is which thing? | What's your name? |
| `Sul en min es Tomi so.` | name of me is Tomi | My name is Tomi. |
| `Pe sin bal fin.` | may you go well | Goodbye. |

Proper names keep their own spelling and are capitalised. They are the only words in
the language exempt from the sound rules.

## Getting by

| Senel | Literally | English |
|---|---|---|
| `Fop o sin so.` | gratitude to you | Thank you. |
| `Min ne map so.` | I not understand | I don't understand. |
| `Resan we.` | say-again [command] | Please say that again. |
| `Min ne mal so.` | I not know | I don't know. |
| `Run we!` | help [command] | Help! |
| `El.` / `Er.` | true / false | Yes. / No. |
| `Huswa em i wan nam he?` | waste-place is at which place? | Where is the toilet? |
| `Dop en tam es ul nam he?` | cost of that is which amount? | How much is that? |
| `Nam yet he?` | which hour? | What time is it? |

## Saying things about the world

| Senel | Literally | English |
|---|---|---|
| `Min nur so.` | I hunger [internal] | I'm hungry. |
| `Min fal pal so.` | I want water | I'd like some water. |
| `Min fat sin so.` | I love you | I love you. |
| `Sem tam es en min lo.` | book that is of me | That book is mine. |
| `Mon bal fa o pin en ren so.` | we-not-you go about-to to building of parent | We're heading to my parents' house. |
| `Hu lol ka du mun ne bal pe.` | if rain ongoing then we-and-you not go | If it's raining, let's not go. |

Note `mon` in the second-to-last line and `mun` in the last: *we excluding you* versus
*we including you*. Senel forces the speaker to choose.
